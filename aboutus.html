About Our Virtual Herbal Garden

Our Mission

We aim to create an interactive and educational space for exploring medicinal plants in a digital environment.

Importance of Herbs

Herbs have been an integral part of human civilization, offering medicinal benefits that support overall well-being. Their therapeutic properties help in treating various ailments naturally.

About AYUSH

AYUSH (Ayurveda, Yoga & Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, and Homeopathy) is an initiative by the Indian government to promote traditional healthcare systems and integrate them into modern practices.

Features

  • Interactive 2D herbal garden
  • 3D plant models for immersive experience
  • Detailed medicinal properties of each plant
